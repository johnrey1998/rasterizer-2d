# AGENTS.md

## Overview
C++20 software rasterizer built with CMake.

## Structure
- `include/rasterizer/math/` & `src/math/`: Math primitives.
- `include/rasterizer/renderer/` & `src/renderer/`: Rasterization routines.
- `include/rasterizer/core/` & `src/core/`: Framebuffer and pixel canvas.
- `include/rasterizer/platform/` & `src/platform/`: Windowing and display output.
- `src/main.cpp`: Entry point.

## Guidelines
- Keep code clean, modern, and minimal.
- Avoid unnecessary comments or boilerplate.
